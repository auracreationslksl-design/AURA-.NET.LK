Service Policies & Governance

Clear, transparent, and professional operational agreements for all our digital solutions.

Web Development Terms

  • 50% advance payment required to start the project; remaining 50% upon completion.
  • Includes up to 2 rounds of basic revisions; additional revisions billed separately.
  • Domain, hosting, and future maintenance charges are separate unless agreed otherwise.

Internet Service Terms

  • Access, speeds, and limits depend strictly on the selected package plan.
  • Extra data usage beyond plan limits will be charged separately.
  • Service provided strictly for lawful, responsible, and authorized use.

Video Editing Terms

  • Raw footage, sound requirements, and script/references must be provided by client.
  • Package includes standard revisions; major structural storyline re-edits incur extra charges.
  • Final rendered videos exported in high-quality format upon full payment.

Photo Editing Terms

  • High-resolution source images must be provided for optimal retouching quality.
  • Retouching covers background removal, color grading, and object manipulation as requested.
  • Final deliverables exported in HD web/print formats; raw source files available on request.
