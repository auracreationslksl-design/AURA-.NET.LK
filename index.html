  • A 50% advance payment will be required before the project will be started.
  • All required content, images, logos, and information will be provided by the client.
  • Up to 2 rounds of basic revisions will be provided.
  • Additional revisions will be charged separately.
  • The remaining 50% payment will be required upon project completion.
  • Domain and hosting charges will be charged separately unless otherwise agreed.
  • Future maintenance and updates will be charged separately.

  • Internet access is provided for lawful and responsible use only.
