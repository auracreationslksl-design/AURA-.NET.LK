  • Client Assets: The client is responsible for supplying all text content, high-resolution media, logos, and credentials in a timely manner.

  • Acceptable Use Policy: Network infrastructure is provided solely for legal operations. Any illegal activities, cyber threats, or bandwidth abuse will result in immediate termination.
